XM6 STUDIO 1.0.1 — START HERE
Native Mac controls for Sony WH-1000XM6 headphones

REQUIREMENTS

• Apple Silicon Mac: M1, M2, M3, M4, or later Apple Silicon.
• macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later.
• Sony WH-1000XM6 headphones, paired with the Mac.
• For Mac/iPhone switching: enable two-device connection in Sony Sound Connect.

This ZIP does not support Intel Macs or older macOS versions. No paid account,
developer tools, Terminal commands, or separate runtime installation is needed.
Bluetooth permission is required. Compatibility on additional Macs has not yet
been physically tested.

UPGRADING FROM XM6 COMPANION

Quit XM6 Companion before opening XM6 Studio. Keep only one version running;
the new name uses the same preferences and headphone assignments. Replace the
old app with XM6 Studio in Applications. Refresh its Bluetooth permission if
macOS requests it. This update changes the name and artwork only.

1. INSTALL AND OPEN

1) Double-click the ZIP to extract the “XM6 Studio” folder.
2) Drag “XM6 Studio.app” into your Applications folder.
3) Open the app from Applications.

This is an independent, locally signed build. It is not signed with an Apple
Developer ID or notarized by Apple, so another Mac may block its first launch.
If you trust the sender and want to open it:

• Try opening the app once, then dismiss the unidentified-developer warning.
• Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
• Find the message about XM6 Studio and click “Open Anyway”, if available.
• Follow the Mac's confirmation prompt, then open the app again if needed.

This creates an exception for this app. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.
If macOS says the app contains malware, is damaged, or your organization blocks
it, stop and obtain a verified replacement or contact your Mac administrator.

Apple's instructions:
https://support.apple.com/en-gb/102445

2. CONNECT YOUR HEADPHONES

1) Open System Settings → Bluetooth. Pair and connect WH-1000XM6.
2) Allow XM6 Studio to use Bluetooth when macOS asks.
3) In XM6 Studio, click “Refresh controls”. Select WH-1000XM6 if needed.
4) Wait for “Controls ready”, battery level and firmware to appear.

If the headphone list is empty while Mac audio already works:

• Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth.
• Turn XM6 Studio off and back on, then choose “Quit & Reopen”.
• Click “Refresh controls” again. This may be needed after replacing a build.

If controls still cannot open, close Sony Sound Connect on the phone and retry.
There is no need to unpair the headphones just to refresh app permissions.

3. SWITCH BETWEEN MAC AND IPHONE

1) On iPhone, open Sony Sound Connect and enable
   “Connect to 2 devices simultaneously”.
2) Connect both the Mac and iPhone to your headphones.
3) Close Sony's app if it prevents the Mac control connection from opening.
4) Click “Refresh controls” in XM6 Studio.
5) Under Overview → Device identities, choose this Mac and your iPhone if the
   app has not recognized them automatically. Choose two different devices.
6) Click “Listen on Mac” or “Listen on iPhone”.
7) Start playback in the chosen device's media app if its music is paused.

“Listen on Mac” also selects the headphones as the Mac's sound output.
A confirmed result means the headphones reported the requested source.
Both devices stay connected for calls. Notifications or phone calls may still
interrupt Mac playback; the app does not continuously force playback back.

4. EVERYDAY CONTROLS

• Sound: noise cancellation, ambient sound, voice focus, EQ presets,
  Background Music, Cinema and DSEE Extreme.
• Headphones: Speak-to-Chat, wearing detection and automatic power-off.
• The headphones icon beside the Mac clock opens quick controls.
• Controls release after 30 seconds without an action. Click “Refresh controls”
  to read the latest settings and edit again. Cached values are labeled.
• Before using Sony's iPhone app, choose “Release controls for Sony app”.
  Return to the Mac app and refresh when finished.
• Closing the window keeps the menu-bar app running. Press Command-Q to quit.
• Optional “Launch at login” is in App settings and is off by default.
• Shortcuts: Command-R refresh; Shift-Command-R release controls.

Firmware updates, custom EQ editing and phone automation remain in Sony's app.
See “Feature Coverage.md” for the complete settings list.

5. TEST STATUS AND PRIVACY

Checked on one Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 15.7.5 with WH-1000XM6 firmware
3.1.5. Twenty-one automated tests passed. Ten Mac/iPhone switches and several
settings changes were confirmed by the headphones. This does not guarantee
uninterrupted playback on every Mac or firmware version. Incoming-call,
notification, sleep/wake and Sony-app coexistence checks remain pending.
See “Verification.md” for the detailed results.

The app works locally without an account, analytics or online services.
Bluetooth device choices are saved separately on each Mac. Diagnostic reports
are exported only when requested and redact names and Bluetooth addresses.
No personal settings or diagnostic exports are included in this sharing ZIP.
